Common questions

What kind of clients does Triple Crown serve?
We specialize in tax and bookkeeping for all types of clients and cases — individuals, solopreneurs, small businesses, and growing companies across every industry. We can help you set up a financial system that will streamline your operations and simplify your tax season, whatever your situation looks like.
What kind of compliance issues can I face?
Tax and compliance requirements vary depending on your state, entity type, and situation. You'll typically need to file state and federal taxes along with any applicable compliance fees, and missing a deadline or filing incorrectly can lead to fines or added complexity — so it's important to file accurately and on time, which is exactly what we help you do.
Why do I need bookkeeping in the first place?
Keeping accurate books matters for any individual or business. It's impossible to file accurate tax and compliance documents without first capturing your transactions, so making sure your books are accurate and up to date is crucial no matter your situation.
Do I have to sign a monthly contract with Triple Crown?
No! We offer monthly subscriptions and one-time tax service depending on what you need!
